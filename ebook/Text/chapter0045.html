35. URDHVA PADMASANA

 

Urdhva Padmasana has fourteen vinyasas, of which the 9th vinyasa is its state.

 

METHOD

Do all vinyasas outlined above through to the state of Sarvangasana; this is the 8th vinyasa. Then, maintaining the same state and doing rechaka, do Padmasana; this is the 9th vinyasa. While in this state, tighten the anus, draw in the stomach fully, hold the knees with the hands, straighten the arms, and slowly do puraka and rechaka as much as possible. Then, doing puraka and rechaka, unlock the legs and come into the 4th vinyasa of Sarvangasana; this is the 10th vinyasa. The subsequent vinyasas are as those for Sarvangasana.
