33. HALASANA

 

Halasana has thirteen vinyasas, the 8th of which is the state of the asana.

 

METHOD

Do the first seven vinyasas described in Sarvangasana. Then, doing puraka, join together and raise the legs, as in Sarvangasana, and doing rechaka slowly, lower the legs to the floor over the head, keeping them straight, place the feet on the floor, lock the fingers behind the back, arms tight and straight, press the chin into the chest without bending the legs, and do puraka and rechaka deeply, as much as possible; this constitutes the 8th vinyasa. The next vinyasas follow those of Sarvangasana. Aspirants should note that rechaka and puraka both occur in the 8th vinyasa.
